## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an elevated, modern Australian career advisory aesthetic. It marries coastal warmth and landscape tones with the precision of executive coaching and migration consulting. The design language serves both Australian local professionals and regional skilled migrants navigating complex visa paths (such as subclass 189, 190, 491, and 494).

The emotional baseline is calm, reassuring, and quietly prestigious. It eliminates bureaucratic anxiety and cognitive noise through ample breathing space, curated typographic scale, and purposeful micro-interactions. The stylistic movement balances high-end minimalism with soft, tactile realism: architectural layouts, generous whitespace, pill-shaped markers, and deeply soft ambient shadows that ground interactive items on sun-warmed linen and sand paper surfaces.

## Colors

The palette references the natural chromatic shifts of the Australian coast at dawn—sun-baked earth, eucalyptus leaves, and oceanic midnight tides:

- **Primary (`#E05A47` - Terracotta Core):** Reserved for primary forward-momentum actions, conversion CTAs, and active journey markers. Used purposefully to highlight critical next steps without inducing stress.
- **Secondary (`#2D6A4F` - Deep Eucalyptus):** Serves as the authority and affirmation anchor. Used for visa qualification flags, verified skill tags, high-confidence match scores, and positive system states. Paired with its soft container tint (`#E8F3EE`).
- **Tertiary (`#6B7B82` - Muted Slate):** Balances interface hierarchy; applied to secondary metadata, visa subclass identifiers, and supporting chart structures.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` - Sand Dawn):** The structural base surface. Provides an inviting editorial canvas that softens eye fatigue compared to pure clinical white. Paired with surface-raised panels (`#FFFFFF`), boundary dividers (`#F0ECE4`), and dark eucalyptus ink typography (`#0E2429` / `#122B32`).

## Typography

The typography uses Plus Jakarta Sans across all roles to achieve a warm geometric clarity that balances high executive professionalism with empathetic approachability. 

- **Headlines:** Set with deliberate negative letter-spacing and strong font weights to establish immediate structure. Essential for anxious candidates parsing complex points tests and job descriptions.
- **Body:** Standardized around a comfortable 16px baseline (`body-md`) with a generous 26px line height. This prevents typographic crowding across multi-paragraph visa prerequisites and coach assessment summaries.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Crisp weights with subtle positive tracking ensure high legibility on compact badges, salary indices, regional location codes, and ANZSCO classification metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column fluid grid system bounded by an executive maximum content width of 1280px on desktop, adapting to a 4-column structure on mobile viewports.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 32px (`2rem`) page margins. Asymmetrical card arrangements split profile overviews (4 columns) from matching opportunities and eligibility scorecards (8 columns).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, and 24px margins. Visual workflows shift into stacked composite panels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins. Navigation shifts to a persistent bottom action sheet; dense tables reflow into digestible, card-based assessment steps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm ambient lighting rather than artificial gray dropshadows, ensuring layers feel natural against the warm sand canvas:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FAF7F2` flat background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Containers):** Solid `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a micro-stroke border (`1px solid #F0ECE4`) and an ambient tinted drop: `0px 4px 20px rgba(14, 36, 41, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Modules / Hovered Cards):** Lifted state with `0px 12px 32px rgba(14, 36, 41, 0.08)` and border transition to `#E8F3EE`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Strategic Drawers):** `0px 24px 48px rgba(14, 36, 41, 0.12)` accompanied by an ambient eucalyptus blur backdrop (`rgba(14, 36, 41, 0.35)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).

## Shapes

The interface balances soft geometry with clear boundaries:

- **Surface Panels & Content Cards:** Fixed at `20px` corner radii (`rounded-xl` equivalent). This provides an inviting, human shape that removes institutional sharpness.
- **Micro-Tags & Action Badges:** Full pill curvature (`9999px`) to frame visa subclass codes, match percentages, and status chips.
- **Interactive Buttons:** Standardized to a cohesive `12px` to `16px` radius, matching the gentle curvature of parent modules without turning overly circular.
- **Inputs & Fields:** Styled with a clean `12px` radius for structural definition and comfortable cursor targeting.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Terracotta `#E05A47` solid background, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `14px`, padding `14px 28px`. Hover elevates with `translateY(-1px)` and shadow `0px 8px 18px rgba(224, 90, 71, 0.28)`.
- **Secondary:** Transparent surface, `1.5px` border in deep eucalyptus `#122B32`, text `#122B32`. Hover shifts background to `rgba(18, 43, 50, 0.05)`.
- **Tertiary / Subdued:** Ghost button with `#122B32` text, no border; hover applies sand tint `#F0ECE4`.

### Chips & Micro-Tags
- **Visa Subclass Badges (e.g., Subclass 491 / 190):** Height `28px`, full pill radius (`9999px`), padding `4px 12px`. Light slate-tinted sand background (`#F0ECE4`), text `#122B32`, label style `label-sm`.
- **Match & Eligibility Indicators:** Eucalyptus container `#E8F3EE`, deep eucalyptus text `#2D6A4F`, accompanied by a leading 6px saturated green indicator dot.

### Cards
- **Opportunity & Pathway Cards:** `#FFFFFF` background, `20px` radius, `1px solid #F0ECE4` outline, padding `24px`. Internal layouts prioritize bold role titles, ANZSCO codes, regional sponsorship status tags, and estimated salary indicators.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #D8D2C7`, height `48px`, radius `12px`, typography `body-md` in `#122B32`. Placeholder in `#6B7B82`. Active focus introduces a `2px` ring in `#2D6A4F` without default browser outlines.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular checks and selectors utilizing `#2D6A4F` when selected, with a smooth spring scale transition. Unselected borders utilize `#D8D2C7` on `#FFFFFF`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Points Calculator Matrix:** Segmented progress ribbon showing state nomination points, regional study credits, and age tiers; uses secondary-tint containers with real-time score updates.
- **Career Pathway Progress Trackers:** Horizontal linked beads that trace milestones from skills assessment submission through state nomination and visa invitation.

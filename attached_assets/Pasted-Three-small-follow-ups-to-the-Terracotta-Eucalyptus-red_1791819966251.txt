Three small follow-ups to the Terracotta & Eucalyptus redesign in artifacts/career-mate. Before starting, run `git pull` so you have the latest main.

1. Dark mode palette
   In constants/colors.ts, only the `light` palette was updated. The `dark` palette still has the old navy/coral values, so users whose phones are in dark mode see the old brand.
   Add a dark version of Terracotta & Eucalyptus to the `dark` object, with every key the `light` object has (same names). Guidance:
   - Backgrounds: a warm near-black, not navy (e.g. background ~#151412, card ~#1F1D1A, muted ~#2A2724, border ~#3A3632).
   - Text: warm off-white foreground (~#F4EFE8); mutedForeground light enough to read on the card.
   - primary: a lighter terracotta that stays readable on dark (around #E07A62), primaryForeground dark (#1C1C19).
   - success/teal: a lighter eucalyptus (~#7FD1A8); successSoft / accent: a deep green (~#1E3A2E) with accentForeground light green.
   - warning / destructive and their *Soft versions: dark tinted backgrounds with light text, same pattern.
   - inkPanel / onNavy / onNavyMuted: keep these working for the dark "Found a role you like?" card and the work-rights panel.
   Every text/background pair you use must have at least 4.5:1 contrast (WCAG AA). Check pairs like primary on card, mutedForeground on background, accentForeground on accent, success on successSoft, warning on warningSoft, destructive on destructiveSoft.

   Change it to: "We'll read the listing and check it against your profile."
   Keep the Seek-specific note that appears when a Seek link is entered (it correctly explains we look for the employer's own copy).

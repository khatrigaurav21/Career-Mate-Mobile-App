---
name: Mobile-first application vision
description: The user's goal for Career Mate is a smooth job-application experience that does not require a PC.
---

Career Mate should make users feel they do not need a PC to apply for jobs.

The user prefers a longer, interactive onboarding with psychologically informed questions that help job seekers recognize how time and PC access affect their ability to apply.

**Why:** The user described this as the experience they want the app flow to deliver and clarified that onboarding should involve customers in reflecting on the problem.

**How to apply:** Review the full mobile journey from bringing in a job through preparing documents, completing the employer handoff, and tracking the application. Be clear about which parts Career Mate prepares and which parts users complete on an employer's site.
